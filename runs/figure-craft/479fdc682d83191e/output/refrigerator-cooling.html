01 The cooling loop

A refrigerator keeps food cold by moving heat out of the cabinet, with refrigerant pumped around a closed loop.

02 The parts

Compressor
Pumps the refrigerant around the loop and raises its pressure.
Evaporator coil
The refrigerant boils here and absorbs heat from the air in the cabinet.
Condenser coil
The refrigerant gives up that heat to the room air outside the cabinet.
Cold air
Cooled air sinks past the food on the shelves.
Door gasket
The seal around the door keeps warm room air out.

The thin pipes between these parts carry the refrigerant. The capillary tube drops its pressure before the evaporator coil.

03 Drawing notes

This is a simplified cutaway. Parts are placed for clarity, are not to scale, and their position varies by model.
