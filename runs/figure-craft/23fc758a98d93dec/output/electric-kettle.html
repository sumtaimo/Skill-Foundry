How an electric kettle boils water

An electric kettle boils water by passing current through a heating element on the floor of its body, which heats the water until the thermostat switches the power off.

Kettle body
Holds the water and keeps the heat in while it boils.
Where it sits: The round steel jug that stands on the power base, with the lid on its open top.Source: General layout of a common cordless electric kettle; not checked against one model.
Water
The water to boil. It is shown clear so the element and thermostat stay visible.
Where it sits: Inside the body, sitting on the heating element; its level is seen through the window in the wall.Source: General layout of a common cordless electric kettle; not checked against one model.
Heating elementAssumed
Heats the water from the bottom when current flows through its coil.
Where it sits: On the floor of the body, under the water; the coil rings sit on its top face.Source: Assumed arrangement: the element is drawn exposed on the floor. Many kettles hide it under the floor.
ThermostatAssumed
Senses the water temperature and switches the element off when the water boils.
Where it sits: On top of the heating element, in the water; it is the small disc at the centre of the floor.Source: Assumed placement: kettles put the boil cut-out in different spots (plate underside, steam path near the spout).
Lid
Closes the top of the body, keeps the steam in and lets you fill the kettle.
Where it sits: Sits on the open top of the body when closed; shown lifted straight up here. A real kettle lid is hinged at the back.Source: General layout of a common cordless electric kettle; not checked against one model.
Handle
The grip you hold to lift and pour the kettle.
Where it sits: Fixed to the outside of the body on one side, from near the top down to the middle, opposite the spout.Source: General layout of a common cordless electric kettle; not checked against one model.
Spout
Directs the water out when you pour.
Where it sits: Fixed to the outside of the body near the top, on the side opposite the handle.Source: General layout of a common cordless electric kettle; not checked against one model.
Power base
Carries the current from the mains cable to the kettle body.
Where it sits: The round base under the body, on three feet; the power cable leaves from its back.Source: General layout of a common cordless electric kettle; not checked against one model.
On/off switchAssumed
Starts and stops the heating.
Where it sits: On the outside of the power base, on its right-hand side below the body.Source: Assumed placement: many cordless kettles put the switch on the base; some put it on the handle.
