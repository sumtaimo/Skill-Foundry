How a refrigerator keeps food cold

A refrigerator keeps food cold by moving heat from inside the cabinet to the coil on its back.

CompressorAssumed
Squeezes the refrigerant gas and pushes it round the loop.

Condenser coilAssumed
The warm coil on the back. The refrigerant gives its heat to the room here.

Evaporator coilAssumed
The cold coil in the freezer. The refrigerant takes heat out of the air here.
Where it sits: On the back wall inside the freezer, behind the ice tray and the frozen food.Source: No product spec, photo or model was supplied. Assumed: the common layout of a household fridge-freezer.
