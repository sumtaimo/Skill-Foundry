How a refrigerator keeps food cold

A refrigerator keeps food cold by moving heat out of the cabinet. Cold air made at the evaporator passes over the food.

Compressor
Pumps refrigerant around the loop. The heat it moves leaves through the condenser coils, usually at the back, which this view does not show.
Evaporator
The cold coil on the back wall. Refrigerant boils inside it and takes heat from the air around it, so the air turns cold.
Cold air
Air chilled at the evaporator. It moves across the food shelf.
Food shelf
Holds the food in the cold air.
Door
Shown swung open. Its seam, handle and hinge are the features drawn on it.
