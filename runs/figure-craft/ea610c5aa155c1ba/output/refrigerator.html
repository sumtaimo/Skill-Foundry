How a refrigerator keeps food cold

Cold air from the vent moves across the shelves, so the food stays cold.

Insulated cabinet
Holds the food in a closed, insulated box. The steel shell and the white liner keep the cold inside.
Door, swung open
Seals the cabinet with a rubber gasket. The inside of the door has bins for small items.
Cold air vent
Cold air comes out of this vent on the inside wall of the cabinet.
Cold air
The moving part. It flows forward from the vent, over the shelves and the food.
Crisper drawer: vegetables
A drawer near the bottom for vegetables. Its handle is on the front rim.
Glass shelves: food and drinks
Milk, cheese and leftovers, meat and yogurt, and bottles of juice, cranberry and water.
