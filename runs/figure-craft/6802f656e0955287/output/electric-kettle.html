How an electric kettle boils water

An electric kettle boils water: a heating element on the floor of the jug heats the water, and when it boils, the steam trips a switch that cuts the power.

Jug
Holds the water and keeps it in while it heats.
Where it sits: The main round body. It stands on the power base, and its open top is closed by the lid.Source: Typical electric kettle layout from general knowledge; no kettle model was supplied.
Lid
Closes the top of the jug. Drawn lifted here, to show the open mouth.
Where it sits: Sits on the top rim of the jug, with a knob and a steam vent on its top. Real lids hinge at the back; this figure lifts it straight up.Source: Typical electric kettle layout from general knowledge; no kettle model was supplied.
Water
The water to be boiled, which the element heats from below.
Where it sits: Fills the jug above the heating element, up to the level the window shows.Source: Typical electric kettle layout from general knowledge; no kettle model was supplied.
Heating element
Turns electric current into heat, which passes into the water.
Where it sits: A flat plate on the floor of the jug, inside it, under the water.Source: Typical electric kettle layout from general knowledge; no kettle model was supplied.
Steam switchAssumed
Steam from the spout bends this metal switch, which cuts the power when the water boils.
Where it sits: Inside the jug near the spout, above the water. Its exact place differs between kettles.Source: Typical electric kettle layout from general knowledge; no kettle model was supplied.
Spout
Pours the water out, and lets the steam out of the jug.
Where it sits: Fixed to the outside of the jug wall near the top, on the side opposite the handle.Source: Typical electric kettle layout from general knowledge; no kettle model was supplied.
Handle
Lets you lift and pour the jug without touching its hot wall.
Where it sits: Fixed to the outside of the jug on the side away from the spout, between the base and the top.Source: Typical electric kettle layout from general knowledge; no kettle model was supplied.
Power base
Carries power from the cord to the jug through the contacts on its top.
Where it sits: The round base under the jug, lifted on feet. The cord leaves it at the back and the contacts sit on its top.Source: Typical electric kettle layout from general knowledge; no kettle model was supplied.
BubbleAssumed
A bubble that forms at the element and rises as the water heats.
Where it sits: An illustration inside the water, above the element. It is not a fixed part of the kettle.Source: Typical electric kettle layout from general knowledge; no kettle model was supplied.
