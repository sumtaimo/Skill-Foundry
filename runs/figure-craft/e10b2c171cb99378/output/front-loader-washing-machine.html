How a front-loading washing machine washes clothes

The motor turns the drum, and the laundry inside is lifted and falls back as the machine washes it.

The parts

Cabinet
The white outer body. It holds every other part, and its back has the power cable, water hose and rating plate.
Door
Swung open on its hinge here. The glass porthole is how you see into the drum when the door is closed.
Control panel
On top of the machine: the display, the program dial and the power light. The detergent drawer sits beside them.
Drum
The steel drum holds the clothes. Its front is open toward the door, and rows of holes run along its wall.
Laundry
The clothes. As the machine washes, they are lifted and fall back.
Motor
Turns the drum. It sits under the drum, inside the cabinet.
