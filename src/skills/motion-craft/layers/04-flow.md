# Layer 04: Flow

1. Check the states of layer 06.
2. Pick the first model in `references/directions.md` whose condition is true.
3. Pick one look from the same file. Project tokens and brand colours replace its palette.
4. Pick two or three craft moves from the same file. Never more.
5. List the facts the user stated, then write 3 to 6 scenes: arrival, what matters (one stated idea each), ending. With one or two facts write exactly 3 scenes: the name, the subject in the user's own words, the name again as the ending. Never make a scene for a fact nobody gave (services, process, materials, visiting), and use at most 3 slots. When the subject has no name (a product or place still to be named), its name is always a slot in the first and last scene, written `[ADD: name]`, beside the subject in the user's words, which stands until it is filled.
6. Give each scene a whole-percent range, what is seen, one main motion with from and to values, its copy, its asset and its still version.
7. Invent one signature motif from the subject (a bottle silhouette, a rim seen from above, a palm frond, a page edge) or use the hero media. It is large, solid and detailed enough to be recognised with no label, and is the thing the visitor remembers: it fills a third of the stage or more and becomes something new in each scene (crop, scale, turn, field). Describe its shape so it can be drawn. Invert the field (ground and text colours swap) in at least one scene, and make the ending a different composition from the opening even when the words repeat.
8. Give each scene one job. Change the composition between scenes (scale, side, density or field colour) so five screenshots do not look alike.
9. Take track length, ranges and text timing from the table in layer 08. M5 keeps natural height, M6 one screen.
10. Give every timing as a percent of the whole track, never of a scene and never in milliseconds (smoothing is the only time value). A stagger is a percent too.
11. Copy what the user gave word for word. Write no other visible copy: no slogan, tagline, invitation or mood line. Ask for such lines under Needs from you.
12. Place text beside or below media. On a phone give each its own band.
13. Write the prompt from layer 08, filling every line from `references/motion-craft.md` that applies.
14. Hand layer 07 the complete reply.
