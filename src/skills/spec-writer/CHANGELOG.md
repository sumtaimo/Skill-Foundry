# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-15

- Agentic-Harness 1.0.47 alignment (under current version pending PO bump approval): added `R-spec-writer-16` (compact `### Flow & State Table` with columns `Step | User does | System shows (loading / empty / error / success) | Next or exit` plus `Entry point:`, `Exit:`, `Reload:`, and `Permissions:` lines for multi-screen/multi-step features; omitted for single-screen stories) and `R-spec-writer-17` (reading `docs/foundation/product.md` and `docs/foundation/domain.md` by reference when present, treating any `(fill me)` line as unknown under `Open questions` / Needs a decision without inventing rules). Added live eval cases `multi-step-flow-table` and `foundation-fill-me-unknown` (14 cases total), re-recorded `evals/portable.json` and `spec-then-retest` recipe.
- First version: acceptance criteria as clauses AC-1 to AC-N in Given-When-Then form, built only from the feature request, user story or requirement text the user gives.
- Ten core layers written as numbered clauses with defined terms; rules R-spec-writer-1 to R-spec-writer-14 in RULES.json.
- Six fixed headings (main path, validation and errors, permissions and roles, empty/loading/disabled states, persistence after reload, limits), each shown only when the text supports a clause.
- Gaps, vague words and conflicts become open questions to the Product Owner; a vague word always gets the measurable-value question and never a number.
- "Out of scope" lists only what the text excludes.
- Fixed sentences for: no requirement text, a title only, a regression checklist for a change or diff (points to test-planner), test code, and design, copy, estimates or priorities.
- Eval case `vague-words` first expected the question to quote "easy to use"; the vague word in the text is "easy" (layer 01 defines a vague word, not a phrase), and the first real answer quoted "easy". The case was wrong on that point and now accepts the quoted word; the skill was not changed.
- Proof: 10 of 10 live eval cases recorded with fresh agents (read-only tools), every source phrase found in its request; portable check and the recipe `spec-then-retest` (spec-writer, then test-planner) recorded with real agents in empty projects.
- Open questions changed (owner decision: fewer questions, and many when they matter). Only a gap that blocks a clause (a missing value, role, state or result without which the clause cannot be written or tested) is kept as a `[blocking]` question; it ends with `Blocks:` and the clause id or the quoted source phrase it blocks. Blocking questions come first and have no fixed cap, per heading or in total. A vague word and a conflict are always blocking.
- All other questions are `[minor]`: merged into at most three lines of at most two questions each, or dropped when they only restate a default (generic error message, loading indicator, what a reload keeps, who else may use it, a heading the text says nothing about). The old limit of three questions per heading is gone. New rule R-spec-writer-15; R-spec-writer-6 and 7 reworded; layers 01, 04 to 10, references and examples updated.
- Two eval cases added: `many-blocking-questions` (many blocking questions are legitimately kept) and `minor-questions-merged` (minor questions merged). `tests/spec-writer.test.mjs` now checks every recording: tags, blocking before minor, every `Blocks:` names an existing clause or a phrase in the request, at most three minor lines of two questions.
- Proof, all by fresh agents (read-only tools): 12 of 12 live evals re-recorded twice (the first set gave minor lines of up to four questions each, so the two-question limit and the drop list were added to the skill, not the cases); portable check and recipe `spec-then-retest` re-recorded. The description did not change, so routing was not re-recorded. Showcase screenshots retaken. In the final recordings the questions per answer fell from 5 to 14 to 0 to 9.
