How a refrigerator keeps food cold

A refrigerator keeps food cold by moving heat from inside the cabinet to the coil on its back.

Compressor
Squeezes the refrigerant gas and pushes it round the loop. It sits in the machine bay at the bottom; turn the fridge to see it.
Condenser coil
The warm coil on the back. The refrigerant gives its heat to the room here.
Evaporator coil
The cold coil in the freezer. The refrigerant takes heat out of the air here.
Cold air
Chilled air falls from the vent and passes over the food.
Freezer
The coldest space, next to the evaporator.
Fresh-food cabinet
Insulated walls keep the cold in and the room heat out.
Glass shelf
Lets cold air pass round the food and shows what is behind.
Crisper drawer
A closed drawer that keeps vegetables from drying out.
Door and bins
A rubber seal closes the gap; bins hold bottles and small things.
