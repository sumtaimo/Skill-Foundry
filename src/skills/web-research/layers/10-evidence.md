# Layer 10: Evidence

1. The URLs and quotes are the evidence: each quote can be found on its page.
2. The tool calls of the session are the record of which pages were read; a URL that is not in them is never a source.
3. "Not fetched" and "Warnings" are part of the evidence; they are never shortened or hidden.
4. Never write that the research is "complete" or "exhaustive"; write how many pages were read, how many failed and how many facts were found.

This is the last layer.
